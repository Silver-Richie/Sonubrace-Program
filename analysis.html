Reading your spectrogram

The methods behind this platform ask that the spectrogram be understandable to anyone, not only to a sonographer. This walks the picture in the order your eye moves across it.

Velocity envelope

Red dots mark each peak systolic velocity; blue dots mark end-diastole. Those two points are where RI, PI and the S/D ratio come from.

How regularly it repeats

The autocorrelation Rxx(τ): how well the trace matches a shifted copy of itself. The dashed line marks the cycle length it found.
